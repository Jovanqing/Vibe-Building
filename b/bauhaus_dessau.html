# Windmill Interlocking Campus

`bauhaus_dessau` &middot; 18.0 m &middot; 6 storeys &middot; T1 = 0.775 s

A **frame idealisation of the massing**, not a model of the built building.
The 5 massing boxes of the source SSOT are meshed by the vibe-building
project's own geometry kernel (`ssot/viz/massing_frame_analysis.massing_to_frame`,
~6 m bays, 3.20 m floors) and drawn by vbviz: 567 nodes,
1128 elements, 7658 t of floor mass (floor area x 6750 Pa / 9.81,
member self weight excluded).

There is **no published structural data for this building to compare against**,
and none is claimed. Nothing here is a validation: the periods below are the
periods of an equivalent frame filling the massing volume, with the sections the
source SSOT's own `frame_design` block carries, and the real building's
structure - its bay sizes, its core, its material, its floor system - is not
known to this model.

## Connectivity

The frame is in **4 pieces**, because the kernel meshes each box independently and these grids miss one another. Nothing was joined that the kernel did not join:

* school+bridge - 186 nodes, z 0.0 to 12.0 m
* workshop - 165 nodes, z 0.0 to 12.0 m
* dorm - 126 nodes, z 0.0 to 18.0 m
* auditorium - 90 nodes, z 0.0 to 8.0 m

The first three modes belong to:

* mode 1, T = 0.775 s - dorm
* mode 2, T = 0.660 s - dorm
* mode 3, T = 0.631 s - dorm

## Reconstruction decisions

None. Every box sits where the source SSOT puts it.

## Warnings

* the frame is in 4 pieces: school+bridge (186 nodes); workshop (165 nodes); dorm (126 nodes); auditorium (90 nodes). That is what the kernel makes of this massing - the boxes' bay grids miss one another - and it is reported, not repaired.
