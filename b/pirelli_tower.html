# Diamond Slab Tower

`pirelli_tower` &middot; 127.0 m &middot; 32 storeys &middot; T1 = 3.418 s

A **frame idealisation of the massing**, not a model of the built building.
The 8 massing boxes of the source SSOT are meshed by the vibe-building
project's own geometry kernel (`ssot/viz/massing_frame_analysis.massing_to_frame`,
~6 m bays, 3.97 m floors) and drawn by vbviz: 2484 nodes,
5340 elements, 25068 t of floor mass (floor area x 6750 Pa / 9.81,
member self weight excluded).

There is **no published structural data for this building to compare against**,
and none is claimed. Nothing here is a validation: the periods below are the
periods of an equivalent frame filling the massing volume, with the sections the
source SSOT's own `frame_design` block carries, and the real building's
structure - its bay sizes, its core, its material, its floor system - is not
known to this model.

## Connectivity

The frame is one connected piece.

The first three modes belong to:

* mode 1, T = 3.418 s - mid_low+left_low+right_low+mid_up+left_up+right_up+roof_cap+core_1
* mode 2, T = 3.374 s - mid_low+left_low+right_low+mid_up+left_up+right_up+roof_cap+core_1
* mode 3, T = 3.245 s - mid_low+left_low+right_low+mid_up+left_up+right_up+roof_cap+core_1

## Reconstruction decisions

* **roof_cap** snapped to the bay grid of mid_up (seated on `mid_up`): footprint [15.0, 4.0, 35.0, 20.0] to [16.0, 6.0, 34.0, 18.0]. the box stood at 120.00 m on a bay grid that missed every node of mid_up; the kernel drops its columns to grade, so it was a free-standing flagpole beside the building. Seating puts its columns on mid_up's nodes.
