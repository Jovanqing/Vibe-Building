# Art Deco Stepped Tower

`park_hotel_shanghai` &middot; 84.0 m &middot; 22 storeys &middot; T1 = 1.098 s

A **frame idealisation of the massing**, not a model of the built building.
The 5 massing boxes of the source SSOT are meshed by the vibe-building
project's own geometry kernel (`ssot/viz/massing_frame_analysis.massing_to_frame`,
~6 m bays, 3.80 m floors) and drawn by vbviz: 1040 nodes,
2340 elements, 12055 t of floor mass (floor area x 6750 Pa / 9.81,
member self weight excluded).

There is **no published structural data for this building to compare against**,
and none is claimed. Nothing here is a validation: the periods below are the
periods of an equivalent frame filling the massing volume, with the sections the
source SSOT's own `frame_design` block carries, and the real building's
structure - its bay sizes, its core, its material, its floor system - is not
known to this model.

## Connectivity

The frame is in **2 pieces**, because the kernel meshes each box independently and these grids miss one another. Nothing was joined that the kernel did not join:

* tower+crown - 592 nodes, z 0.0 to 84.0 m
* podium+core_1+core_2 - 448 nodes, z 0.0 to 22.0 m

The first three modes belong to:

* mode 1, T = 1.098 s - tower+crown
* mode 2, T = 1.027 s - tower+crown
* mode 3, T = 0.693 s - tower+crown

## Reconstruction decisions

* **crown** snapped to the bay grid of tower (seated on `tower`): footprint [12.0, 9.5, 28.0, 25.5] to [14.0, 7.5, 26.0, 27.5]. the box stood at 60.00 m on a bay grid that missed every node of tower; the kernel drops its columns to grade, so it was a free-standing flagpole beside the building. Seating puts its columns on tower's nodes.

## Warnings

* the frame is in 2 pieces: tower+crown (592 nodes); podium+core_1+core_2 (448 nodes). That is what the kernel makes of this massing - the boxes' bay grids miss one another - and it is reported, not repaired.
