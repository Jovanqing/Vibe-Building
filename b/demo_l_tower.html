# demo_l_tower - an L-plan office tower, designed by the agent

`demo_l_tower` &middot; Shanghai &middot; 50 storeys &middot; 220 m &middot;
42 x 42 m envelope, one corner notched &middot; frame-core &middot; T1 = 5.352 s

## What this is

A 220 m, 50-storey concrete office tower on an L-shaped plan: two wings inside a
42 x 42 m envelope with the north-east corner cut away, a single central core,
1.55 m square columns, 3213 nodes and 8450 elements at 6 m bays and
4.4 m floors, 201,481 t of mass (floor load plus RC self weight).

It was **designed by the agent, not by hand**. The episode
`demo_l_tower__vibe-pipeline__r0.json` finished PASS in one C2 round
(`_trace` has round 0 and nothing after it, 8 solver evaluations) with
**no topology change** (`_topo_changed: false`): the core is part of the
frame-core system chosen at B2, not a repair bolted on after a failure. The
whole returned design is six numbers - column side 1.55 m, one core at
15% of the plan with aspect 1.0 and no offset, zero walls, beam scale 1.0,
modulus scale 1.0, taper 0.0 - and this folder is that design rebuilt call for
call by `build_model.py` and drawn by vbviz.

## The governing constraint

The **global stability index** (刚重比, `EJd/(H^2 * sum Gi)`, JGJ 3-2010 5.4.4).
Limit **1.400**, achieved **1.4122** - a 0.9 % margin, and the only one of
the five checks anywhere near its limit:

| check | limit | achieved | margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| story drift ratio | 0.0017 (the harness target; JGJ 3 3.7.3 gives 0.001775 for frame-core at 220 m) | 0.00082 | 2.1x |
| shear-weight ratio | 0.012 | 0.010724 | -10.6 %, within the 20 % that JGJ 3 4.3.12 lets you cover by amplifying the seismic action 1.119x |
| torsional period ratio Tt/T1 | 0.90 | 0.6439 | 1.4x |
| **global stability index** | **1.400** | **1.4122** | **+0.9 %** |
| top acceleration | 0.25 m/s2 | 0.1489 m/s2 | 1.7x |

That is what fixes the columns at 1.55 m: stability, not strength and not drift.
Drift has a factor of two in hand.

## Two honest notes

* **The L notch is assumed, not given.** The brief says "L-shaped plan, 42 x 42 m
  envelope" and no more. The harness's own geometry synthesiser cuts a corner of
  40 % x 40 % and tags the record `geom_source: "exact+L_assumed40"` - and its
  own rule is that records carrying an `assumed` tag must not be used for
  torsion conclusions. The torsional displacement ratio (1.2325) and period
  ratio (0.6439) here are properties of that assumed notch, not of the brief.
* **The comfort check uses the 10-year wind.** JGJ 3-2010 3.7.6 asks for the
  10-year return pressure, which the original brief did not carry; the field
  `basic_wind_pressure_10yr_kpa = 0.40` was added to this record for the demo
  (0.40 kPa is Shanghai's value under GB 50009, the same figure the four
  Shanghai instances in the library use). Against the 50-year 0.55 kPa the top
  acceleration would be 0.2047 m/s2 instead of 0.1489, still inside 0.25 but
  with much less room.

## Reproduction

`build_model.py` asserts three numbers against the episode file, exactly as it
was returned:

| quantity | episode | rebuilt |
|---|---|---|
| nodes | 3213 | 3213 |
| elements (`len(cols) + len(beams)`) | 8450 | 8450 |
| T1 | 5.352164 s | 5.352164 s |

Periods T1..T6: 5.352, 4.660, 3.446, 2.262, 1.502, 1.359 s. The third, 3.446 s, is the torsional one the
episode reports as `T_torsional_s`. Sum of support reactions 1,976,525 kN
against the episode's `sigma_G` 1,976,525 kN.

## The two drawings

`figures.py` writes two orthographic drawings beside the camera pictures, both
300 dpi on the studio theme, both measured back off the saved image into
`figure/figures.json`.

**`figure/plan.png` - typical floor framing plan, level 25 of 50, z = 110.0 m.**
The L outline, the 63 columns of 1.55 m, the 106 beams at 6 m bays and the
12.6 x 12.6 m core. *The core bracing is an X in the plane of each core face,
so in plan it is only the dashed yellow line lying along that face - the X
itself is in the elevation.* Measured back off the image: the drawn plate is
41.85 x 41.88 m against the 42 m envelope, 0.3 % on each axis.

**`figure/elevation.png` - north elevation, the lower ten storeys.**
A partial elevation, cut by a break symbol, at 26.1 px/m - 115 px per storey,
enough that the columns, the floor beams and the core's X-bracing are each
separately legible. The full 42 m x 220 m elevation does not survive a
column-width figure: 50 floor lines land about a pixel apart and moire into a
ribbon, and the frame gets a third of the sheet. The lower ten storeys are
42 x 44 m, near square, and carry the same three facts - the two wings and
where the notch takes one of them away, the braced core, the 4.4 m storey. The
frame is identical all the way up, as the break note says. The full height is
still drawn to true scale, as vector, in `studio/elevation.pdf`, and as a
cutout in `cutouts/model_elevation.png`.

**On the core share.** `figures.json` reports `core_share` 0.104 while the
design parameter is `area_ratio` 0.15. The three numbers have three
denominators and none of them is wrong: `run_pipeline.core_layout` takes 0.15
against the **first massing box only** (42 x 25.2 m = 1058.4 m2), which gives
158.8 m2 of core; that same core is 10.7 % of the whole 1481.8 m2 L floor
(`core_share_geometric`); and the pixel count reads a shade lower again, 10.4 %,
because the beams and columns drawn over the core zone count as members rather
than as core. The model is untouched.

## What is here

* `figure/` - the two orthographic drawings above plus `figures.json`, their
  own measurements read back off the saved images
* `studio/` - the vbviz default product set plus `preview/`, `index.html` and
  `manifest.json` ([open](studio/index.html))
* `cutouts/` - isometric, elevation and plan on the studio theme plus a
  studio_white isometric, 3000 px, RGBA, no ground plate
* `process/` - `build_4view.mp4` (1920 px) and `build_iso.mp4` (1080 px), the
  frame assembling itself from the ground up, with posters
* `results.json` - the three checks, the design parameters, the five targets and
  what the design achieved, the model's own periods and mass, and the folder
  read back off disk
