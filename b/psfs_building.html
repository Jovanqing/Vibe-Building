# T-Shape Steel Office Tower

`psfs_building` &middot; 150.0 m &middot; 43 storeys &middot; T1 = 4.010 s

A **frame idealisation of the massing**, not a model of the built building.
The 7 massing boxes of the source SSOT are meshed by the vibe-building
project's own geometry kernel (`ssot/viz/massing_frame_analysis.massing_to_frame`,
~6 m bays, 3.50 m floors) and drawn by vbviz: 1762 nodes,
3468 elements, 16197 t of floor mass (floor area x 6750 Pa / 9.81,
member self weight excluded).

There is **no published structural data for this building to compare against**,
and none is claimed. Nothing here is a validation: the periods below are the
periods of an equivalent frame filling the massing volume, with the sections the
source SSOT's own `frame_design` block carries, and the real building's
structure - its bay sizes, its core, its material, its floor system - is not
known to this model.

## Connectivity

The frame is in **2 pieces**, because the kernel meshes each box independently and these grids miss one another. Nothing was joined that the kernel did not join:

* base+tower_wing+tower_upper+top_crown+core_1+core_2 - 1546 nodes, z 0.0 to 150.0 m
* tower_stem - 216 nodes, z 0.0 to 60.0 m

The first three modes belong to:

* mode 1, T = 4.010 s - base+tower_wing+tower_upper+top_crown+core_1+core_2
* mode 2, T = 3.925 s - base+tower_wing+tower_upper+top_crown+core_1+core_2
* mode 3, T = 1.983 s - base+tower_wing+tower_upper+top_crown+core_1+core_2

## Reconstruction decisions

* **top_crown** snapped to the bay grid of tower_upper (seated on `tower_upper`): footprint [20.0, 15.0, 30.0, 25.0] to [22.0, 16.667, 28.0, 23.333]. the box stood at 110.00 m on a bay grid that missed every node of tower_upper; the kernel drops its columns to grade, so it was a free-standing flagpole beside the building. Seating puts its columns on tower_upper's nodes.

## Warnings

* tower_upper stands at base_z 60.00 m on tower_stem but overhangs its bay grid by up to 7.5 m more than half a bay; it is an overhang, not a box seated on the one below, so it is left exactly as the source draws it. Its columns drop to grade, beamless over the drop - the kernel's rule for a box in the air.
* the frame is in 2 pieces: base+tower_wing+tower_upper+top_crown+core_1+core_2 (1546 nodes); tower_stem (216 nodes). That is what the kernel makes of this massing - the boxes' bay grids miss one another - and it is reported, not repaired.
